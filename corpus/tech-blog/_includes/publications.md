### Knowledge Graph Enhanced Recommender Systems

**B.Sc. Thesis** · Aalto University, School of Science · September 2025 · *Graded 5/5 (Excellent)*

A literature review comparing traditional recommender systems (collaborative filtering, content-based filtering, hybrid methods) against knowledge graph-enhanced approaches (graph neural networks and knowledge graph embeddings). KG-based systems address the cold-start problem and improve explainability through path-based reasoning, at the cost of higher computational overhead and construction complexity.
